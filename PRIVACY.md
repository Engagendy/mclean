# Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026

theMClean is a macOS cleanup review tool. It scans local files on your Mac to help you review cleanup candidates before staging or moving selected items to Trash.

## Data Collection

theMClean does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data.

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or remote telemetry.

## Local File Access

theMClean runs locally on your Mac. Scan results, settings, staged item records, and cleanup history are stored locally on your device.

The app may request access to folders you choose or to macOS-protected locations when you grant permission. File access is used only to scan, preview, stage, restore, or move selected files according to actions you perform in the app.

## Network Access

theMClean does not require an account and does not send scan results or file metadata to a server.
